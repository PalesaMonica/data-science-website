How to get involved

The group is aimed at undergraduate students across the CSAM and EIE schools. You do not need any data science experience to start.

  1. Come on a Thursday. We meet from 13:15 to 14:00 in MSL005.
  2. Enrol on Moodle. Session material lives on the Mathematical Sciences Moodle course.
  3. Start reading. The training manual covers everything from the first session onwards.
